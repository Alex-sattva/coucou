HOME + CHAT island split

The island's two surfaces have swapped jobs. HOME (house) is where the agents tell you what needs you — a permission, a failure, a question, a finished run — one line at a time, never a queue of cards. CHAT (bubble) is the voice harness: the big Mochi you talk to, the voice bar, the conversation. Same card, same vocabulary. ▶ Demo tour plays the whole arc, in motion, in the first capture.

What is the harness, what is re-bound five captures · one vocabulary

Kept verbatim

  • Palette and springs — :root and --spr untouched: #000 island, #141518 card, #F5F6F8 ink, #8E939C / #6B7079 dim, the agent colours.
  • One body — the canvas Mochi class, STATE, hopPose, IC and one requestAnimationFrame loop. The face is the state: 1.4 s blink, breathe, 900 ms hop, 950 ms roll, 1.6 s ping, 1 s spin.
  • One line — .slot holds either the .ticker (30 pt lines scaled, current line shimmering 2.2 s, 450 ms cubic-bezier(.3,.9,.3,1) roll, neighbours #5F646D) or the .summary sliding in on sumIn.
  • One riser — .riser from max-height:0 on --spr, the original's .si / .txt / .acts / .abtn strip, amber 6 % tint.
  • One accent per agent — .adot, .halo (--hc, .42, blur 10), .card.left::before (--wash, 22 %), .pring, the pill tint (13 % / 32 %), the .wave bars.

Re-bound at island size

  • The surfaces swapped jobs — HOME is the agents' one-line report plus the roster; CHAT is the Mochi + voice bar plus the conversation list. Two surfaces, one card.
  • Type inside 640 × 160 — the base scale is re-pointed onto the 98 pt box: name 11.5, ticker 10.5 / 11.5, cap 10.5, riser 10.5 / 9.5, pill 10.5 / 8.5.
  • The ticker centres on the current line — .tk-in{top:50%} with translateY(−(i·13 + 6.5)px), so the bright line stays centred whether the slot is 21 pt (riser open) or 41 pt (at rest).
  • The caption is one line — .vbar .cap small goes inline instead of stacking, so the four resting controls (thread, expand, +, mic) survive the shorter bar.
  • Two colours come back to the agent — .summary .sdot from --ok to --ac, and the pill's working ring from #3B9EFF to --c.
  • CHAT is the flexible surface — .real98.tall is 640 × 212 (150 pt box), the only box where the conversation can scroll.
Why it is still the same app: nothing is enumerated. There is one Mochi, one self-replacing line, one composer, and a riser that exists only while a decision does. HOME never counts what is behind the line, and CHAT never adds a second vocabulary — the conversation is the history's own rows, given the whole card.
